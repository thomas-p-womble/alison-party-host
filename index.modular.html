    iPhone opens the YouTube app by default — that login does not reach this party page. You must sign into YouTube inside Safari.

    Do this once:

    1. Open Safari
    2. Tap the address bar and type youtube.com (don’t tap a YouTube link — tapping opens the app)
    3. Sign in with Premium in Safari if needed
    4. Settings → Safari → Prevent Cross-Site Tracking → Off (turn back on after the party)
    5. Paste the party link in Safari and play

    “Open Google Account” uses accounts.google.com so iPhone is less likely to steal you into the YouTube app. After you’re signed in, open the party link in Safari.

    Write & Show

    What is this?

    Every girl writes the answer on a wipe board / sticky note (no buzzing). When you hit the button, the phone counts 3 · 2 · 1 · SHOW! and everyone holds up their board at once. Award points to everyone who’s right.

    How to run it

    1. Give each girl a wipe board + marker (or sticky + pen)
    2. Ask a question (or play a short Name That Tune clip)
    3. Give ~15 seconds to write
    4. Tap 3 · 2 · 1 · SHOW!
    5. Kids flip boards up together — you score

    Freeze Dance

    How to run Freeze Dance

    1. Kids dance while music plays
    2. Tap PLAY — starts a dance song
    3. Tap FREEZE — music pauses; anyone still moving is out
    4. Tap Eliminate 1 when someone moves; repeat until a winner

    Hot Potato / Prize Pass

    How to run Hot Potato / Prize Pass

    1. Kids pass a prize/potato while music plays
    2. Tap Start Round — music plays ~15–20s then stops by itself
    3. Whoever is holding it when it stops is out (or wins that prize)
    4. Tap Next Round for another song

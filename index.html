    YouTube Premium (no ads)

    iPhone opens the YouTube app by default — that login does not reach this party page. You must sign into YouTube inside Safari.

    Do this once:

    1. Open Safari
    2. Tap the address bar and type youtube.com (don’t tap a YouTube link — tapping opens the app)
    3. Sign in with Premium in Safari if needed
    4. Settings → Safari → Prevent Cross-Site Tracking → Off (turn back on after the party)
    5. Paste the party link in Safari and play

    “Open Google Account” uses accounts.google.com so iPhone is less likely to steal you into the YouTube app. After you’re signed in, open the party link in Safari.
